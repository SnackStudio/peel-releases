Safe by design

An archive can't reach outside its folder.

Archives can be built to write files where they shouldn't. Peel checks every entry before it writes anything.

  • Stays in the folder you chooseEntries that point outside it, like ../ or absolute paths, are refused.
  • Never follows links outSymbolic links can't redirect a write somewhere else.
  • Signed and notarizedChecked by Apple, and every update is signed too.

Peel Pro Coming soon

Locked out? Find a way back in.

Forgot the password to an old backup? Inherited an archive nobody can open? Peel Pro is guided recovery for the archives you can't get into.

Opening, browsing and extracting stay free forever. Pro only adds recovery.

  • Free check firstPeel tells you what it can learn about a locked archive, and whether recovery is realistic, before you pay for anything.
  • Remember, don't configureAnswer a few plain questions about the password you used. Peel turns them into a focused search, with no masks or wordlists to set up.
  • Look for the originalsSometimes the fastest way in is a copy you already have. Peel checks your Mac for the files the archive was made from.
  • Stays on your MacRecovery runs locally. Your archive and your clues never leave your computer.
