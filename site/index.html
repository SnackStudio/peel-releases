Peel

Open what's inside.

A free unarchiver for your Mac. Double-click a ZIP, RAR, 7z or TAR archive and browse it like a folder in Finder, or extract it right where you are.

Download Peel 1.0.0 brew install --cask snackstudio/tap/peel

macOS 13 or later · Apple silicon and Intel · Notarized by Apple · Updates itself

Browse like a folder

Double-click an archive and Peel opens it in the same Finder window. No clutter, no extra steps.

Extract from Finder

Right-click with Peel, Peel to… or Peel & Reveal. Encrypted archives ask once for the password.

Quick Look inside

Press Space on an archive to see what it contains before you open it.

Safe by design

Peel never writes outside the folder you choose or follows links out of it, whatever the archive contains.

Formats

ZIP and ZIP-based files (EPUB, JAR, APK, CBZ, Office documents), RAR including multipart sets, 7z, and TAR with gzip, bzip2, xz or zstd, plus single gzip, bzip2, xz and zstd files.
