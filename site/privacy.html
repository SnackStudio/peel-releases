Privacy policy

Peel for macOS · Snack Studio · Effective October 14, 2026

The short version

Peel does not collect, store or share any personal data. Archives you open and the files inside them stay on your Mac.

What Peel does with your files

Peel reads the archives you open and writes their contents where you choose, or into its own folder on your Mac when you browse an archive. All of this happens on your Mac. Peel never uploads your files, their names or their contents anywhere.

Network connections

  • Mac App Store edition: makes no network connections. Updates come through the App Store.
  • Edition downloaded from this site: checks peel.snackstud.io/appcast.xml for updates. Peel asks before it checks automatically, and you can check by hand from Peel → Check for Updates… instead. The check sends nothing about you or your Mac beyond what any web request carries, such as your IP address and the app's version. The site's host keeps standard request logs to operate the site; Snack Studio does not use them to identify or track anyone.

Analytics, tracking and accounts

Peel contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and has no accounts.

Permissions Peel asks for

Peel asks for access to a folder only so it can extract archives into it, and remembers that choice on your Mac. The edition downloaded from this site can also ask to control Finder, so double-clicking an archive opens it in the same Finder window. You can change both at any time in System Settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@snackstud.io. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
